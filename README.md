# Making climate information useful for farming

Climate. Crops. Decisions.

I’m Victor Nyabuti Ong'era, a PhD researcher working on climate-resilient farming systems. I use crop models, seasonal forecasts, remote sensing, and environmental data to study practical adaptation choices.

[See my research](research.md) · [Read my CV](cv.md)

## Current work

I’m interested in the point where climate data stops being an abstract projection and starts helping someone make a decision.

- Climate-resilient farming systems — using crop simulation models and climate information to support agricultural adaptation.
- Remote sensing for crop and disease monitoring — linking field observations, phenology, and crop modelling for wheat systems.
- Wheat adaptation under climate change — downscaling climate projections and testing adaptation options for wheat systems.

## Research interests

- Crop simulation models
- Seasonal climate forecasts
- Climate-resilient farming systems
- Remote sensing for agriculture
- Environmental data analysis for decision-making
